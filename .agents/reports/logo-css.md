Cinzel Regular 400 is self-hosted as fonts/cinzel-regular.woff2 (Cinzel Regular 2.00).
Source TTF: https://github.com/NDISCOVER/Cinzel/raw/master/fonts/ttf/Cinzel-Regular.ttf
License source: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/cinzel/OFL.txt
License path: fonts/OFL.txt (SIL OFL 1.1).
Google Fonts ofl/cinzel ships variable Cinzel[wght].ttf only; official static Regular used instead.
Converted with host /usr/bin/woff2_compress; TTF was not left in fonts/.
@font-face loads only the local woff2. No fonts.googleapis.com.
The + is a 5rem square .logo-mark; ::before is the horizontal bar, ::after the vertical bar, both #FFFFFF at 15% thickness.
Wordmark is Cinzel 1.15rem, tracking 0.4em with matching padding-left, white, 0.85rem under the mark.
Header margin-top is 2.25rem so the plus has air. Logo colors are #000000 and #FFFFFF only.
