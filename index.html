Surmount designs high-assurance compute infrastructure: defense in depth across software and hardware, provably hardened. We research Bitcoin infrastructure and cryptographic protocols. Original work is public domain under the Unlicense.

The Census classifies that work as NAICS 541512 Computer Systems Design Services: planning and designing computer systems that integrate computer hardware, software, and communication technologies.

Self-improving AI platforms

We are building Grok OSS, a userspace harness that will help us bootstrap a provably perfect OS (best we can, despite everything). The OS we aim to build is argued on Philosophy. That OS is designed not to need a reboot after updates; that is an aim, not a proven result. The aim is to unify the browser and the OS into efficient, safe OS-level primitives.

Advanced computing technologies

Quantum computing, optical compute, and neuromorphic in-memory compute are research aims, not shipped products.
