Bitcoin and monetary sovereignty

Bitcoin is a direct challenge to nation-state monetary sovereignty + a voluntary check on central banker overreach.

Bitcoin is a peer-to-peer electronic cash system, named on Grokipedia Bitcoin and in Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System (Satoshi Nakamoto). A central bank is the institution that issues currency and conducts monetary policy.

First option versus hyperinflation

If we want to avoid the violence and bloodshed typical of hyperinflation, Bitcoin must be the first option.

That is a company claim about how we want the transition to go. Hyperinflation is a collapse of a currency's purchasing power; historical cases are named on that Grokipedia page, not invented here.

Fiat, 1971, and debt jubilee

The dollar as we know it has only been that way since 1971. Fiat systems end as those before them ended. We may now have the technology to learn from history. We want the demise of the dollar to be a joyous debt jubilee, not chaos. Debt jubilees every fifty years should return as a matter of course.

That 1971 sentence is our historical reading of the Nixon shock and the end of dollar convertibility into gold: monetary history on this page, separate from the Leviticus 25 verses printed below. Fiat money is government-issued money not convertible to a commodity. The Bretton Woods system and the gold standard are the monetary order that reading names. See also Nixon and the End of the Bretton Woods System, 1971 (Office of the Historian) and Gold Convertibility Ends (August 1971) (Federal Reserve History).

The biblical jubilee is Leviticus 25: the fiftieth year of Jubilee, when remission is proclaimed and every man returns to his possession. That law is distinct from the 1971 historical reading. A debt jubilee is the broader name for ancient and later debt cancellation. Quoted scripture on this page is Original Douay-Rheims, not an encyclopedia paraphrase.

Natural law

We work toward a hardened, defensible monetary system, built on natural law. On this site science is natural philosophy: look at nature without flinching, and keep claims inside the scientific method. Applied mathematics + cryptography are emergent properties of nature. They are extrajudicial.

The encyclopedia entry we cite is natural law.

Voluntary system

We hold that a voluntary system is the fitting solution. Bitcoin is the natural choice, and it still has flaws. We must make it more secure + more private + more scalable + work for us.

That restates the peer-to-peer cash design in Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System and the Bitcoin article: no trusted third party as the monetary base. Flaws remaining is why the work on Projects exists.

Protection and the OS we aim to build

If our contributors and friends and loved ones are most protected against all conceivable threats to the things they value, we will endure where others fail. That is one of the most important company philosophies. The OS we aim to build is the most absurdly paranoid and hardened OS that is still computationally efficient.

Grok OSS is a userspace harness that will help us bootstrap a provably perfect OS (best we can, despite everything): that same paranoid and hardened OS that is still computationally efficient. See Grok OSS.
