Humility

Humility is meekness of heart: taking up the yoke and learning because Christ is meek, and humble of heart. The words we quote are Matthew 11:29:

Take up my yoke upon you, and learn of me, because I am meek, and humble of heart: and you shall find rest to your souls.

Matthew 11:29, Original Douay-Rheims

What the virtue does is count others better than oneself:

Nothing by contention, neither by vain glory: but in humility, each counting other better than themselves:

Philippians 2:3, Original Douay-Rheims

That is the moral virtue. It is not a scientific verdict on theism or atheism. Holy Lean records both site quotations as equal to the Douay-Rheims source strings. Humility is warranted by those two verses, Matthew 11:29 and Philippians 2:3, not by a fourth one-verse pairing. The three one-verse warrants are false testimony, peace-makers, and Cherubins. See isomorphism.

Theism

Theism is a strong claim: that we live in a universe with a divine, omniscient creator who will judge each person for their actions. From intellectual honesty, the theistic position lacks sufficient evidence. It should not be considered proven according to the scientific method.

Atheism

Atheism is also a strong claim. It should not be considered proven according to the scientific method.

Science

Science is natural philosophy. Science works by a loop of theory and evidence. See scientific method.

Scientific method

Methodological reasoning about empirical observations made of nature that either validate or invalidate theories and models. Science works by a loop of theory and evidence. We do not consider the scientific method to be contradictory to anything stated in scripture unless there is proof to the contrary. Russell's teapot is a named example on Faith: a point about scientific method, not a proof of God.

Scripture

Named scripture on this site includes the Ten Commandments (Exodus 20), the Beatitudes (Matthew 5), and Genesis 3. Quoted words are the Original Douay-Rheims, not King James: Old Testament, English College of Douay, 1609; New Testament, English College of Rheims, 1582. Dataset: cryptoquick/original-douay-rheims (unmodified historic translation, not Challoner). We treat the lessons of the Ten Commandments and the Beatitudes as a shared moral framework we will operate under. We read Genesis 3:24, with Philo of Alexandria, as a picture of the theory-and-evidence loop, not as a laboratory result. Surrounding prose is American English. Quoted spelling stays as printed.

Cherubins

Genesis 3:24 in this edition says Cherubins (plural), Paradise of pleasure, and a flaming and a turning sword:

And he cast out Adam, and placed before the Paradise of pleasure Cherubins, and a flaming and a turning sword, for to keep the way of the tree of life.

Genesis 3:24, Original Douay-Rheims

Holy Lean maps the vocabulary term cherubins to that citation and those words. Later English cherubim, the Akkadian gloss karibu, and Philo are separate notes. They are not this quotation.

Cherubim

Later English often writes cherubim. That is not the printed Douay-Rheims spelling in Genesis 3:24. The Grokipedia Cherub page gives Hebrew kərūḇ, plural kərūḇīm (keruvim), and the usual English plural cherubim. That page does not quote the 1609 Douay text. We keep Cherubins in the verse we quote.

Karibu

The word karibu is not in this Douay-Rheims edition. It is a later historical gloss, not a Douay-Rheims quote. Other spellings in the same discussion include Karubim and kāribu.

The Grokipedia Cherub page proposes an Akkadian background: karābu, "to bless" or "to pray"; kāribu (diminutive kurību) as intercessory spirits; kirubu, "great" or "mighty." The same page compares those figures to shedu and lamassu gate guardians at temples and palaces. William F. Albright, on that page, rejects a direct Akkadian loan. The etymology is debated. It is not proven.

That is comparative history. We do not say the Cherubins of Genesis are lamassu, and we do not say Genesis is Enuma Elish. Enuma Elish is a Babylonian seven-tablet combat creation (Marduk and Tiamat). That Grokipedia page notes parallels with Genesis. It does not name cherubim. The Garden of Eden page, in later English, says God stationed cherubim and a flaming sword to guard the way to the tree of life from the east. The words we quote remain Paradise of pleasure, Cherubins, and a flaming and a turning sword.

Philo

Philo of Alexandria was a Hellenistic Jewish philosopher who read the Pentateuch by allegory. That Grokipedia page is biography and method. It does not treat On the Cherubim.

C. D. Yonge's English of the treatise is titled A Treatise on the Cherubim; and On the Flaming Sword; and On the First-Born Child of Man, Cain. In one allegory there, the two figures symbolize goodness and authority, and the flaming sword is the symbol of reason, "prompt and fiery reason." That English is Yonge, not the Douay-Rheims. A Grokipedia Holy of Holies summary of temple cherubim as divine wisdom and guardianship is that page's summary, not Yonge's wording.

On this site we read those two Cherubins as the Angel of Theory and the Angel of Evidence, working conjunctively in the theory-and-evidence loop. That picture is ours. It is not Philo's wording and not a Douay-Rheims quote.

Isomorphism

The warrant story is one type: Warrant is either oneVerse or versePair. False testimony, peace-makers, and Cherubins each use oneVerse (Exodus 20:16, Matthew 5:9, Genesis 3:24). Humility uses versePair of Matthew 11:29 and Philippians 2:3. That is not a fourth one-verse pairing.

An isomorphism here is a Lean pair of inverse functions with proofs (Iso, the same shape as Equiv) and theorems that the site quotation equals the Douay-Rheims source string. The one-verse fragment maps false testimony, peace-makers, and Cherubins onto those three citations. That fragment is not the whole warrant story. That is not an isomorphism between science and scripture, and it does not prove that God exists.

Sin

A useful definition of sin is the darkness, lacking in God's good light, and rejection of reason and truth. That is a site definition. It is not a Douay-Rheims quotation and has no scripture isomorphism.

Bitcoin

Bitcoin is a direct challenge to nation-state monetary sovereignty + a voluntary check on central banker overreach. If we want to avoid the violence and bloodshed typical of hyperinflation, Bitcoin must be the first option. Bitcoin is the natural choice, and it still has flaws. That wording matches Philosophy. It is not extra theology. See Grokipedia Bitcoin and Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System.

Fiat

The dollar as we know it has only been that way since 1971. Fiat systems end as those before them ended. That 1971 sentence is a site historical reading of the Nixon shock. It is not a Douay-Rheims quotation. See Grokipedia Fiat money.

Grok OSS

Grok OSS is a harness. It is not yet an operating system. It will help us bootstrap the OS we aim to build.

Post-quantum cryptography

Post-quantum cryptography is classical cryptography designed so that known attacks on a quantum computer do not break it. On this site that means BIP 360 (proposed) and libbitcoinpqc. It is not quantum computing and not a quantum computer.

Quantum computing

Quantum computing is a class of machine. Optical compute and neuromorphic in-memory compute are other machine classes. They are research aims, not shipped products. None of those machines is BIP 360 or libbitcoinpqc.

Provably perfect

On this site, provably perfect means the best we can, despite everything: logical consistency of our printed claims and of the OS we aim to build. That is not a proof of metaphysical perfection, that God exists, or that theism is proven.

Endure

If our contributors and friends and loved ones are most protected against all conceivable threats to the things they value, we will endure where others fail. That is printed company philosophy. Lean does not prove that Surmount will endure in the world. Lean does not prove that Bitcoin will win.
